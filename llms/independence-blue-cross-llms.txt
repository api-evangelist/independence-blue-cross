# Independence Blue Cross (IBX)

> Independence Blue Cross (IBX) is a health insurer serving Philadelphia and southeastern Pennsylvania. IBX offers individual and family health plans, Medicare plans, employer-sponsored coverage, member services, care resources, and information for brokers and health care providers.

Individual and family health plans from IBX are available to eligible Pennsylvania residents in Bucks, Chester, Delaware, Montgomery, and Philadelphia counties. Plan availability, benefits, provider networks, drug coverage, costs, and eligibility can vary by plan, location, and plan year. For decisions about a member's specific coverage, use the applicable plan documents or member account, or contact IBX.
